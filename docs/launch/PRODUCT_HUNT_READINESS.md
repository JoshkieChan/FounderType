## Product Hunt Positioning

### One-line Description

Identify why you keep stopping, put your next commitment on public record, and keep the record honest for 30 days.

### Short Description

FounderType is a behavioral commitment system for repeat starters. It names your stopping pattern, records one public 30-day commitment, and makes follow-through visible.

### Maker Comment Draft

FounderType exists because I spent years starting things, stopping, starting again, and privately ignoring the pattern.

Most tools give people more information. FounderType does something different: it identifies the specific behavior pattern behind the stopping, makes one commitment public, and keeps the record honest through check-ins and accountability receipts.

It is intentionally not a course, coaching app, or motivation feed. If someone is not willing to put one commitment on record, they are probably not the user.

The goal is simple: help repeat starters finish one real thing without hiding the moment they quit.

## Pre-Launch Verification

- Production URL opens without auth wall.
- `/`, `/questions`, `/results`, `/commitment`, `/feed`, `/privacy`, and `/terms` are reachable.
- Public commitment flow is usable.
- hCaptcha is configured.
- Privacy and terms pages are reachable.
- No waitlist-only state.
- No placeholder or demo-only copy in the user-facing launch funnel.

## Do Not Do

- Do not ask for fake upvotes.
- Do not relaunch a duplicate product page.
- Do not make the product unavailable during launch day.
- Do not describe FounderType as a quiz, course, or generic productivity app.
